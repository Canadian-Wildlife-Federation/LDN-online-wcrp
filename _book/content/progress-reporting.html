Progress Reporting

This WCRP was initiated in 2023. The initial development of the WCRP included portions of the Quesnel, Bowron, and Cariboo watersheds. Several scoping exercises were completed by the planning team to help guide the initial development of the WCRP, including a threats assessment that detailed the extent, severity, and overall threat of all five barrier types in the Bowron, Quesnel, and Cariboo (see Appendix D). Situation analyses were also completed by the planning team to identify factors that contribute to the prevalence of culverts and dams on the landscape (see Appendix E). In 2025, the geographic scope of the WCRP expanded to include portions of the Cottonwood watershed. Since 2023, the passability of 97 structures has been assessed, and further habitat assessments were completed at 2 of these.

Rehabilitated Barriers

No barriers were rehabilitated through the implementation of this WCRP as of May 2026.

Annual Progress Report

Since March 2025, the following actions were completed: - The WCRP geographic scope was expanded to include the Cottonwood watershed. The national hydro network layer was processed to develop an initial spawning and rearing model for Pacific salmon. Satellite imagery was reviewed to exclude structures that could be visibly verified to be a bridge, not exist, or were cross ditches that did not connect to a stream.

  • LDN fisheries technicians completed assessments of structures in the LDN watersheds. Field results still need to be reviewed and incorporated into the model.

  • CWF provided LDN planning team members with QGIS (free and open-source geographic information system software) technical support and onboarding, as well as training to: complete guided and independent PSCIS and satellite review of modelled-stream crossings in QGIS, fix submissions via CSV, and run a tributary-by-tributary mapping exercise for the Cottonwood and digitize data into QGIS.
